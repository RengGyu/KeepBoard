Description
In this fast and goofy family game, 2 to 4 players begin by simultaneously rummaging through the common warehouse, frantically trying to grab the most useful component tiles for building their space ship—all in real-time.
Once the ships are launched, players will encounter dangerous situations while vying for financial opportunities—each hoping to gain the most valuable cargo and finish with as much of their ship still intact as possible.
Of course, that's easier said than done since many hazards will send pieces of your ship, your cargo, and your crew hurling into the depths of space.
The goal is to survive the trek (hopefully with at least some of your crew and ship intact) and have at least one credit by the end of the game (a profit, yay!). Players earn credits by delivering goods, defeating pirates, having the best-looking ship, and reaching their destination before the others.The ageless classic has relaunched! It's modernized, faster, more accessible, and just as fun as ever!

Terms and conditions for resolving any new complaints of damaged games during transport:
iva@czechgames.com
Reach us and provide following documents and pictures:
We need a scan of a document stating that the shipment arrived damaged.
Provide a picture of a whole pallet with the damage.
Provide a picture of the damaged games (ex. 3 damaged Codenames = one picture with 3 Codenames where the damage is obvious).
We will evaluate the damage and issue a credit note for you:
We do not accept complaints when only the game foil is damaged a bit.
If there is only a box damaged and the game itself is still ok, you receive 50% discount.
If the game is damaged a lot, you receive 80% discount.
If you need assistance or have any questions regarding our brand manual, please email us.
