Description
Expand your farms and mines to gain the resources to build your cities. This lays the groundwork for technological advancements, better governments, and great wonders. Choose wise leaders whose legacy will lead your people to greatness. Strengthen your army to protect your borders and to expand your territory. And shape history with your political skill.
A strong economy, advanced technology, happy citizens, enlightened government, a strong military - your civilization needs to do well in many areas. You may excel in some, but you should not fall behind in others. Adjust your strategy to the opportunities the game offers. Your rivals will seek advantages through wars and politics, so keep your defenses up. But don’t neglect the finer things in life, because the civilization with the most influential culture wins.

Terms and conditions for resolving any new complaints of damaged games during transport:
iva@czechgames.com
Reach us and provide following documents and pictures:
We need a scan of a document stating that the shipment arrived damaged.
Provide a picture of a whole pallet with the damage.
Provide a picture of the damaged games (ex. 3 damaged Codenames = one picture with 3 Codenames where the damage is obvious).
We will evaluate the damage and issue a credit note for you:
We do not accept complaints when only the game foil is damaged a bit.
If there is only a box damaged and the game itself is still ok, you receive 50% discount.
If the game is damaged a lot, you receive 80% discount.
If you need assistance or have any questions regarding our brand manual, please email us.
brand@czechgames.com
