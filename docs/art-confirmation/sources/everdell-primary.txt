Description
Everdell
is a delightful worker placement game that features a
detailed world realized through stunning artwork. Utilizing a
tableau-building and worker placement mechanic, players will
work to build a city of critters and constructions. Everdell is easy to
learn, yet offers satisfying strategic depth and endless replayability.
Everdell Essentials Edition
allows you to experience Everdell at a more affordable
price point with cardboard components.
